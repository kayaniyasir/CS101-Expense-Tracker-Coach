## How marking works

- **Full:** required programming evidence is correct and the student explains the relevant work.
- **Partial:** proportionate credit for partly correct work and understanding.
- **Zero:** required evidence or relevant understanding is absent.
- Individual marks may differ between team members.
- Practice quizzes are **formative and ungraded**. The project is worth **10 course marks in total**.

## Showcase

Bring your NetBeans Java source project, a **2–3 page report**, a **roughly 3-minute team demonstration**, and be ready for a **roughly 2-minute individual explanation, trace and modification**. Team size is 4–5 students. All team members contribute code and tests.

Source: CS101 Project Showcase Student Guide and Instructor Guide, Project 3 (Personal Expense Tracker).
